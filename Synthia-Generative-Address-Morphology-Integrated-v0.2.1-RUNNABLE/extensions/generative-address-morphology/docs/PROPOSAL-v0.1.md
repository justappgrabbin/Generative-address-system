# Proposal: Synthia Generative Address & Morphology Engine

Working Proposal v0.1

This package treats the proposal supplied by Adaya as the architectural foundation. The canonical text is intentionally preserved as project doctrine rather than rewritten by the integration layer.

## Central Thesis

> Synthia is a generative intelligence built from an already-addressed multidimensional field. Every meaningful structure occupies a position within that field. Semantic and spatial relationships determine which connections are valid; dimensional laws determine how those connections behave; neural morphology stabilizes and computes their interactions; and language, imagery, sound, timing, embodiment, and action emerge as parallel expressions of the resulting configuration.

## Build invariants captured from the proposal and current architecture

- Everything has an address.
- Accuracy precedes trait assignment.
- Address is location plus computational context.
- Five dimensions are distinct fields, not duplicated copies.
- Semantic relationship and dimensional/spatial geometry jointly matter.
- Meaning composes progressively and recursively.
- Relationships form before networks stabilize them.
- Gates can carry latent morphology specifications; valid connections instantiate functional graph structures.
- Cross-layer connectivity is conditional.
- Awareness center and timing are computational context.
- Person-specific activations produce person-specific graph ecology.
- Language is both read and write interface.
- Experimental mappings remain explicitly experimental until supported.
- Cynthia remains intact. This engine is additive and connects to her; it does not rewrite her.
- Graphs are structural objects with no hands or intent. They may compete structurally for active coverage/expression while people/processes cooperate.
- A graph exists only while its formation conditions hold.
