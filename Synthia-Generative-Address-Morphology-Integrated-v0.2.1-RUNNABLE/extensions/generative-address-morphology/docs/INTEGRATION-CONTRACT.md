# Integration Contract

1. **Cynthia is not edited.** The original r21.21 chassis is preserved byte-for-byte in `vendor/cynthia-original/00-Synthia-Core-Organism-r21.21.zip` and extracted only for runtime import.
2. All new behavior lives in this additive engine or adapters.
3. Existing safety mechanisms, hands, heartbeat, autonomy, training, execution and self-maintenance remain intact.
4. Donor packages are preserved untouched under `vendor/donors/`.
5. Connection definitions, connection rules, morphology implementations, graph dynamics and projections are separate registries so later discoveries can be added without restructuring Cynthia.
6. Graphs have no hands. They constrain and carry mechanism. Hands remain faculties of participating processes/agents.
7. Graph activation is conditional. When required conditions fail, the live graph dissolves; provenance remains.
8. People/processes are not ranked as winners. Dominance is a property of active graph-energy coverage/expression.
9. No experimental mapping silently becomes canonical. Every rule or morphology carries evidence status and provenance.
